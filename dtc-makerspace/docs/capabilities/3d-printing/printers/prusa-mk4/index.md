The Original Prusa MK4 is an open Cartesian (bed-slinger) 3D printer available in the DTC office.

## Quick Links

- [Wireless Printing Access Request](https://forms.osi.office365.us/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=ornV8r1eukKGkKENa77QhaitGsMoaXhKjfcIL8FHNQRUMFZJSk9KUDUyTE1RU1ZGU0xXSTlRNTZKQi4u): The MK4 supports Wi‑Fi printing, but wireless access is restricted. Submit this form to request permission to print wirelessly.
- Contact [Ryan Kuederle](mailto:ryan.kuederle@udri.udayton.edu) for assistance or to report issues.

## Getting Started
    
If you have not used the Prusa MK4 before, or otherwise need help with 3D printing, contact [Ryan Kuederle](mailto:kuederler1@udayton.edu).

After you are set up, follow the [MK4 Standard Operating Procedure](sop.md) during regular use.

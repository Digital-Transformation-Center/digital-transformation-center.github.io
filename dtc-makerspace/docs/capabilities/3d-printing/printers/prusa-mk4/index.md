The Original Prusa MK4 is an open Cartesian (bed-slinger) 3D printer available in the DTC office.

## Quick Links

- [Wireless Printing Access Request](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSckJDdSpQWUWbNIRqO0h6LokrG6Q7IQ2_-ZudXRN2CUwgkYFw): The MK4 supports Wi‑Fi printing, but wireless access is restricted. Submit this form to request permission to print wirelessly.
- Contact [Ryan Kuederle](mailto:kuederler1@udayton.edu) for assistance or to report issues.

## References

- [PrusaSlicer](https://www.prusa3d.com/p/prusaslicer/)

## Getting Started

If you have not used the Prusa MK4 before, or otherwise need help with 3D printing, contact [Ryan Kuederle](mailto:kuederler1@udayton.edu).

After you are set up, follow the [MK4 Standard Operating Procedure](sop.md) during regular use.
